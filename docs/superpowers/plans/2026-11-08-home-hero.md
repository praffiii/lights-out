# Home Hero (Ground + Tracker) Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Replace Home's photo hero and the separate circuit band with one hero. It shows the next race's circuit on its real ground, drawn with contour lines. A light laps the track, and the photo beside it shows whichever of the 22 2026 cars is lapping.

**Architecture:**
- One R3F canvas shows the calendar-picked circuit.
- The contour ground is a GLSL port of the Blender look.
- Two refs drive the scene:
  - `fill`: lights out tweens it 0→1 over 1.6 s;
  - `tilt`: hero scroll progress, which moves the camera only.
- A pure `lapProfile()` turns the circuit's centreline into a speed-true lap clock, scaled to 14 s.
- When the clock wraps at the start line, the lap counts as done: the `Tracker` beside the canvas moves to the next car, and its 22 ticks double as the gallery index and the lap timer.

**Tech Stack:** Same as `2026-11-08-home.md`: TanStack Start on Workers, React 19.3, R3F 9.8 with three 0.187, GSAP 3.15, `bun test` with happy-dom, Playwright 1.64, and Blender 5.2 run headless.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-07-f1-fan-site-design.md`. Also read `DESIGN.md` (the 7 rules and "per circuit: built once; per race: data only") and `docs/motion-map.md`.

  | Light | `#EFEEE9` | `#7E8187` | `#EDEBE6` |

- **Contour spacing:** one line per metre of real ground. Heights are exaggerated ×3, so `STEP = 3` model units.
- **Lap physics:**
  - lateral grip 44 m/s²;
  - top speed 92 m/s;
  - acceleration 11 m/s², braking 38 m/s²;
  - curvature measured over ±6 samples;
  - the displayed lap is always 14 s.
- **Motion timings:**
  - the fill runs 1.6 s with `power2.inOut`, starting on the loader's lift;
  - the lap starts when the fill ends;
  - the photo wipe is 0.9 s `expo.out`, moving in the car's direction of travel (right to left).
- **Pausing (WCAG 2.2.2).** The lap and the auto-advancing gallery stop when:
  - the visitor presses Pause;
  - the hero is off screen;
  - the tab is hidden.

  Under reduced motion nothing moves on its own: car 01 shows, the sectors are filled and there is no light.

### Task 3: Contour ground shader

**Files:**
- Modify: `src/circuits/shaders.ts`, `src/circuits/Circuit.tsx` (ground material only)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces: `contour(theme: Theme, span: number) => { material: THREE.ShaderMaterial; setTheme(theme: Theme): void }`.

There is no WebGL in `bun test`. **[my call]** This task is verified by eye against the poster, not by a unit test. Review Focus 1 still covers the shader's failure path.

- [ ] **Step 1: Implement `contour()`.**
  - **Line position.** `h = worldPosition.y`, and `f = fract((h + 3.0 * 0.37) / 3.0)`.
  - **Line width.** `line = 1.0 - smoothstep(0.0, fwidth(h / 3.0) * 1.2, min(f, 1.0 - f))`. Because the width comes from screen-space derivatives, lines stay hairline on flat ground, which is what removes the shelf smear.
  - **Radius fade.** `pow(1 - min(r / (span * 0.75), 1), 1.6)`, where `r` is the xz distance from the track centre.
  - **Haze.** Mix in the haze colour by view distance, with near and far at `span × 2.7 × 0.7` and `span × 2.7 × 1.9`, and an exponent of 1.4.
  - **Colours.** Use the Global Constraints palettes.
  - **Wiring.** In `Circuit.tsx`, the `ground` mesh gets this material instead of the matte one.
- [ ] **Step 2: Compare by eye.** Run `bun run dev`, open `/` in both themes, and compare the canvas with `public/circuits/br-1940-{dark,light}.png`. Expected: the same contour density, no smear near T12, and lines fading towards the frame edges.
